## Overview

We study spectral behavior and approximation on the unit sphere by linearized shallow networks with analytic activations. Asymptotics for ultraspherical coefficients yield sharp Sobolev approximation rates for spectrally compatible functions, together with explicit coefficient bounds and analytic convergence rates.
