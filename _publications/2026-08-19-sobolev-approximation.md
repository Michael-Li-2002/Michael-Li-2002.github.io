## Overview

We study Sobolev approximation on bounded domains by linearized shallow neural networks with prescribed inner parameters. For a class of analytic activations, quasi-Chebyshev parameter sets and ridge-function lifting yield sharp approximation rates, with parameters distributed over fixed intervals.
